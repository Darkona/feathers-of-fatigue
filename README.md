# Feathers of Fatigue

**Stamina for Minecraft, as a row of feathers above the food bar.** Sprinting, jumping and whatever other mods choose cost feathers. When they run out you can push on for a while, at a price.

![Feathers above the food bar, with a golden row of Endurance feathers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Darkona/feathers-of-fatigue/master/wiki/images/hud.png)

- **Strain**: past empty, you keep going on red strain feathers. They are paid back first and slowly, so overdoing it leaves you drained.
- **Rest**: standing still, crouching or sitting pays strain back faster. Sleep restores everything.
- **Weather and climate**: cold slows recovery, heat makes everything cost double, and the Nether, fire and lava cut your maximum feathers.
- **Heavy armor and mounts** (optional): armor holds back feathers, and horses, donkeys, mules and camels have feathers of their own.
- **Potions**: Endurance, Energy, Momentum and Cooling.

It works out of the box with Droplets of Thirst, Serene Seasons, Curios, Jade and AppleSkin, among others, and other mods can spend feathers through its API.

![Riding a camel: its feathers, in its colors](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Darkona/feathers-of-fatigue/master/wiki/images/mount-camel.png)

**Minecraft versions:** 1.21.1 on NeoForge and 1.20.1 on Forge. The [wiki](https://github.com/Darkona/feathers-of-fatigue/wiki) has every mechanic with its numbers, the configuration, and what each [Minecraft version](https://github.com/Darkona/feathers-of-fatigue/wiki/Minecraft-Versions) supports.
